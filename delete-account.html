Delete Your Account

App Name: Career Pilot: ATS AI - Resume

Developer: WeaverSol LLC

You can ask us to delete your Career Pilot account and the data linked to it at any time. You do not need the app installed to make this request.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to m.irfanshkh@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Career Pilot account.
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to Career Pilot (email sign-up or Google sign-in), so we can confirm the account is yours.
  3. We will reply to confirm, and delete the account within 30 days of your request.

Data that is deleted

  • Your account: name, email address, profile picture and user ID
  • Your resumes, cover letters and job description matches
  • Your AI credit balance, credit history and AI usage records
  • Resume improvement suggestions and in-app activity linked to your account

Unused AI credits are lost when the account is deleted and cannot be refunded.

Data that may be kept

  • Records of purchases are kept by Google Play and our payment processor (RevenueCat) as required for tax, accounting and fraud prevention. We keep a record of the order ID only for as long as the law requires.
  • Copies in encrypted backups are removed automatically within 30 days.
  • Resume data saved only on your device is not stored by us. Remove it by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.

See our Privacy Policy for more details about the data we collect.
